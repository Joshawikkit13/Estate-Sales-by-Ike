Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Estate Sales by Ike

Estate Sales by Ike ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit ikesestatesales.com or subscribe to our email and SMS sale alerts.

1. Information We Collect

When you voluntarily submit our subscription form, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, email address, and mobile phone number.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To send upcoming estate sale announcements, dates, locations, and reminders via email or SMS text message (based on your selection).
  • To respond to customer inquiries sent via phone or email.

3. Mobile Information & Third-Party Sharing

No mobile information, phone numbers, or SMS opt-in data will be shared with, sold to, or rented to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties, except with service providers (such as our messaging platform) strictly necessary to deliver the text messages you requested.

4. Your Choices & Opt-Out Rights

You may unsubscribe from email alerts at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email. You may opt out of SMS text alerts at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at estatesalesbyike@gmail.com or call (918) 319-4853.
